Reflection

1. How do I approach designing software?
  I used a top-down approach, where I broke down complex requirements into much smaller, modular components. There, I then focused on planning the structure and logic first to ensure there is a stable foundation.
2. What new design skills has your work on the project helped you to craft?
  I developed spatial reasoning and from the ground up, 3D visualization skills. I learned how I can twke everyday items and deconstruct them to their more primitive shape and apply coordinate transformations effectively.
3. What design process did you follow for your project work?
  I followed a visual to technical workflow. The reference photo that we took at the beginning, I took that and mapped it onto a 2D grid, built a wireframe prototype in OpenGL, and then I added the fun stuff, like lighting and textures to add a touch of realism.
4. How could tactics from your design approach be applied in future work?
  Using the tactic of incremental refinement, which is building a functional low-poly version before adding more complex features, that is applicable to any software project. This ensures core functionality works before turning the focus on user interface and aesthetics.
5. How do I approach developing programs?
  Followed an iterative and modular approach. I broke the program down into functional blocks, such as initialization, input handling, and rendering. This ensured that each piece works correctly seperately before integrating it into a larger system where they work together.
6. What new development strategies did you use while working on your 3D scene?
    GPU- side programming is what I utilized while using GLSL shaders and managed memory through vertex buffer objects (VBOs). I also practiced 'mathematical debugging,' where I adjusted matrices and vectors to fix visual clipping and perspective issues.
7. How did iteration factor into your development?
   It was essential. I started with rendering single shapes with solid colors, then worked toward more complex meshes, then finally adding lighting and texture to the scene. This allowed logic errors to be caught before they were harder to find and fix.
8. How has your approach to developing code evolved throughout the milestones, which led you to the project’s completion?
   My appraoch evolved from hard coding simple values to creating reusable functions and classes. By the last milestone, I moved from trial and error and was able to begin using a more calculated approach with camera controls and light poloshing.
9. How can computer science help me in reaching my goals?
    Computer science provides the analytical and technical framework needed for more complex problems. The tools I have and will continue to gain will help me build scalable solutions and automate tasks.
10. How do computational graphics and visualizations give you new knowledge and skills that can be applied in your future educational pathway?
    They bridge the gap between abstract mathematics and visual feedback. Understanding how to translate linear algebra and geometry into rendered images can help provide a strong foundation for future studies in AI, data science, or advanced simulation.
11. How do computational graphics and visualizations give you new knowledge and skills that can be applied in your future professional pathway?
    These skills allow me to create intuitive data interfaces and immersive environments. Professionally, being able to visualize complex data or develop VR/AR apps makes me be a more versatile developer in industries that range from software engineering to digital design.
